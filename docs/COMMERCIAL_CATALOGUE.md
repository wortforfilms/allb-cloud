# MAATAA × Siddham proposed commercial catalogue

Status date: 2026-08-29

## Commercial boundary

All prices in this document are proposed launch prices. They are not existing
public prices, accepted orders, invoices, production availability or a promise
of future delivery. Prices exclude GST, cloud consumption, travel,
third-party hardware and separately contracted legal or support obligations.

The repository currently supports controlled technical pilots and evaluation
work. It does not support production claims for enterprise identity, AAL3,
approvals, signed releases, air-gapped production, physical NPU performance or
ASIC IP.

| Status | Commercial meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Quoteable pilot | May be proposed only as a controlled, scoped engagement with explicit acceptance criteria and limitations |
| Evaluation | Research or development access without production certification |
| GA candidate | Packaging/licensing candidate; not GA until its release gates pass |
| Planned | Do not accept a normal production order |
| Service | Human-delivered engagement governed by a statement of work |

## Pricing policy

- Annual subscription: proposed 15–20% discount to monthly billing.
- Three-year enterprise agreement: maximum additional 12% discount.
- Indian academic institution: proposed 60% discount.
- Recognized research/nonprofit organization: proposed 30% discount.
- Government: milestone-based quotation, never self-service.
- OEM royalty: proposed 3–7% of product value or fixed per-device fee.
- Perpetual licence: approximately 3–4× annual licence plus maintenance.
- Pilot credit: up to 50% toward the first annual enterprise licence.
- Production SLA, source access, escrow and indemnity are separate.
- No lifetime unlimited API or hardware-IP licence.

## Evidence-to-sale gate

No SKU advances to GA merely because it appears in this catalogue. The
required release evidence is defined in
[`PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md`](PRODUCT_ROADMAP.md). Commercial status changes require:

1. implemented and tested entitlements;
2. matching licence and legal terms;
3. security and operational acceptance;
4. signed and reproducible artifacts;
5. truthful product claims;
6. named human approval with immutable release evidence.
